ScanGov already scores most U.S. government sites. Look yours up to see what the policy will fix.

What you get

  • Purpose and scope: what the policy covers.
  • Policy statement: an outcome, a floor, and practices for accessibility, botability, security, usability, and readability.
  • Roles: who owns, writes, builds, buys, and delivers.
  • Measurement: a scan schedule, a fix deadline, and a public scorecard.
  • Procurement, exceptions, and review: how new work launches and how the policy changes.
  • Adopted standards: the five ScanGov Standards categories, linked, with the adoption date.

Buying a new site? The request for proposal (RFP) builder writes the same standards into the contract.
