About the policy generator

Most organizations have an accessibility policy and nothing else. This generator writes one policy for everything you publish online. It uses the ScanGov Standards as the baseline.

What the policy includes

  • Purpose and scope: what the policy covers
  • Policy statement: an outcome, a baseline, and practices for botability, accessibility, security, usability, and readability
  • Roles: who owns, writes, builds, buys, and delivers
  • Measurement: how you check against the baseline and fix failures
  • Procurement, exceptions, and review: how new work launches and how the policy changes
  • Adopted standards: the five ScanGov Standards categories, linked
