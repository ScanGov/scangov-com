Plain language helps people find answers and act on them. Readability is now a ScanGov indicator, so you can see which pages are hard to read and track your progress as you rewrite them.

## What it does

ScanGov scores the main text of every page with the Automated Readability Index (ARI) and the Flesch–Kincaid readability tests. A page passes when its ARI, rounded, is at or below your site's reading level target.

## How it works

- Set your own reading level target for each site in Settings. The default is grade 8.
- Review every page with its ARI and Flesch–Kincaid scores. The ARI card comes first, because ARI decides pass or fail.
- Score changes show up a few minutes after you save a new target. You do not need to rescan.
- See how federal, state, county, city, and education sites compare on the new [readability rankings](https://scangov.org/rankings/readability/).

The <a href="https://docs.scangov.org/readability/">readability docs</a> explain how it works.

## Also new across ScanGov

- The [readability scan](/tools/readability/) and the [good bot scan](/tools/good-bot-scan/) now look and work the same way, and so will future tools.
- The new [Flesch–Kincaid readability tests](https://standards.scangov.org/flesch-kincaid/) standard page explains the formula.
- The rankings cards now show each group's average score and how many sites we scanned.
- The Settings page has a Save button for your reading level.

## Try it

Check any public page with the free [readability scan](/tools/readability/). To track every page on your site, log in to your ScanGov dashboard, or read more on the <a href="/features/readability/">readability feature page</a>.
