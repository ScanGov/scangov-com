We built the <a href="/tools/policy-generator/">digital experience policy generator</a> to help governments write one policy for everything they publish online.

## What it does

Most organizations have an accessibility policy and nothing else. This policy also covers AI and search readiness, security, usability, and plain language. It names an owner, sets a scan schedule, and sets a deadline to fix failures.

The [ScanGov Standards](https://standards.scangov.org/) are the baseline, so the policy gives your team a clear standard to work toward.

## How it works

- Enter your organization
- Choose options, such as the laws to cite and the reading level
- Copy, print, or edit the policy

Nothing you enter leaves your browser.
