We built the <a href="/tools/policy-generator/">digital experience policy generator</a> to help governments write one policy for everything they publish online. It starts from a <a href="/tools/policy-generator/#sample">sample policy</a> that you can customize.

## What it does

Most organizations have an accessibility policy and nothing else. This policy also covers AI and search readiness, security, usability, and readability. It names an owner, names the standards you work toward, and says how you monitor.

The [ScanGov Standards](https://standards.scangov.org/) are the baseline, so the policy gives your team a clear standard to work toward.

## How it works

- Read the sample policy
- Enter your organization
- Customize it: the laws to cite, the standards to name, the reading level, and how you monitor
- Copy, print, download, or edit the policy
