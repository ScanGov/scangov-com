How we compare

  • Every claim about another tool links to a public source with the date we checked it. We re-check each quarter.
  • Where we could not confirm a capability, the cell says so. We do not fill gaps with guesses.
  • Where another tool has something ScanGov does not, such as FedRAMP or authenticated scanning, the row says so.
  • ScanGov runs next to your current tool. None of these pages ask you to drop one.
